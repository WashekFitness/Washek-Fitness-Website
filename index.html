FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is WASHEK really free?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and has no time limit. It includes your personalized 12-week program, the live workout tracker with auto rest timer, a free Weekly Update, and 100 Coach Kael messages a month. Paid plans (Progress, Performance, and Elite) are optional upgrades that add more Kael messages and extra features, and they are billed monthly.

Do I need a gym or equipment?

No. You tell WASHEK what equipment you have, from nothing but a floor to a full gym, and your program is built around it. Calisthenics programs can run on a pull-up bar and your bodyweight.

I'm a complete beginner. Is WASHEK for me?

Absolutely. Beginner programs start with safe foundational movements, and Kael explains cues in plain language with no textbook jargon. As you progress, the program progresses with you.

What styles of training does WASHEK support?

Four: calisthenics, weighted calisthenics, traditional weight training, and hybrid training, which puts skill work first while your nervous system is fresh and finishes with weights.

How does Form Lab work?

Record or upload a video of a set. Form Lab analyzes it to count reps, time static holds, flag form issues, and suggest corrective exercises. The results are AI-generated and are not a substitute for an in-person coach. Form analysis is part of the Elite plan.

How accurate are the AI body fat and food scans?

They are estimates, not lab or medical measurements. Photo and barcode scans give an approximation that can help you track trends, but they can be wrong, so treat them as guidance. Food scanning starts on the Progress plan, and body fat estimates start on the Performance plan.

Are my workout data and progress photos private?

Your logs, body metrics, videos, and photos belong to you. We never sell your data, and your private training videos and progress photos are never made public. You can request export or complete deletion of your account and associated media by contacting support.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time from your Profile in the app. Access stays active through the end of your prepaid billing period.

Does WASHEK replace a doctor or a human coach?

No. Coach Kael is an AI, not a human coach or medical professional, and WASHEK does not provide medical advice. Individual results vary. Talk with a physician before starting a new training or nutrition program, especially if you have an injury or health condition.
