Questions

Washek Fitness, explained.

Quick answers for people deciding whether the app fits the way they train.

What is Washek Fitness?

Washek Fitness is a fitness app that combines personalized training, nutrition tools, progress tracking, and AI coaching in one place.

Is Washek Fitness for calisthenics?

Yes. The app supports calisthenics, weighted calisthenics, weights, and hybrid training approaches.

Can I use Washek Fitness with home equipment?

The app uses the equipment information you provide when shaping your training, making it suitable for different training setups.

What is Kael?

Kael is the AI coaching experience inside Washek Fitness, designed to provide training-focused guidance using available app context.

Does Washek Fitness include nutrition tracking?

Yes. Nutrition tools include macro tracking and food or package scanning features.

Does Washek Fitness track progress?

Yes. The app includes workout progress and analytics tools, as well as progress photo tracking.
