About

What WASHEK is, and why it exists.

WASHEK (Weighted Athletic System for Hybrid & Elite Kalisthenics) is an AI-based workout builder and fitness tracker designed to eliminate guesswork. I built WASHEK because I know how frustrating it is to put in maximum effort and still hit a plateau.

Managing progressive overload, tracking macros, identifying weak points, and maintaining a structured program—all at once—can feel overwhelming. WASHEK brings every aspect of your training together in one place. From building custom workouts to tracking your progress and nutrition, you get total visibility over your fitness.

Plus, with Kael checking in post-workout and delivering your weekly reports, you’ll always know exactly where you stand and what to improve next.

FAQ

Questions about WASHEK.

Clear answers about the fitness app, training styles, nutrition tools, progress tracking, and Kael AI coaching.

What does WASHEK stand for?

WASHEK stands for Weighted Athletic System for Hybrid & Elite Kalisthenics.

What is WASHEK?

WASHEK is a fitness app that brings personalized training, nutrition tools, progress tracking, and AI coaching together in one place.

Is WASHEK a calisthenics app?

Yes. WASHEK supports calisthenics, weighted calisthenics, weights, and hybrid training approaches.

Can WASHEK build a personalized workout plan?

WASHEK uses the training information you provide, including your goals, experience, equipment, schedule, and preferences, to shape your training experience.

Does WASHEK include nutrition tracking?

Yes. WASHEK includes nutrition tools for macro tracking and food or package scanning.

What is Kael AI?

Kael is the AI coaching experience inside WASHEK, providing training-focused guidance using the context available in the app.

Can I track my workouts and progress?

Yes. WASHEK includes live workout tracking, workout history and progress tools, analytics, and progress photo tracking.
