FAQ

Questions about Washek Fitness.

Clear answers about the fitness app, training styles, nutrition tools, progress tracking, and Kael AI coaching.

What is Washek Fitness?

Washek Fitness is a fitness app that brings personalized training, nutrition tools, progress tracking, and AI coaching together in one place.

Is Washek Fitness a calisthenics app?

Yes. Washek Fitness supports calisthenics, weighted calisthenics, weights, and hybrid training approaches.

Can Washek Fitness build a personalized workout plan?

Washek Fitness uses the training information you provide, including your goals, experience, equipment, schedule, and preferences, to shape your training experience.

Does Washek Fitness include nutrition tracking?

Yes. Washek Fitness includes nutrition tools for macro tracking and food or package scanning.

What is Kael AI?

Kael is the AI coaching experience inside Washek Fitness, providing training-focused guidance using the context available in the app.

Can I track my workouts and progress?

Yes. Washek Fitness includes live workout tracking, workout history and progress tools, analytics, and progress photo tracking.
